# El Patrón - Preliminary Research Summary
**Client:** El Patrón Mexican Restaurant
**Location:** Salisbury, NC
**Date:** 2026-01-08
**Research Phase:** Discovery & Analysis
**Status:** CRITICAL - Revenue Recovery Required

---

## Executive Summary

El Patrón faces a critical revenue decline of **-$450K (-18.9%)** over 3 years, dropping from $2.38M (2023) to $1.93M (2025). This research reveals that **the decline is NOT a market problem—it's an execution problem**.

**Key Finding:** El Patrón operates in a LOW-COMPETITION market (only 2-3 direct Mexican competitors) with clear market dominance (#1 by review volume: 1,383 reviews), yet is underperforming on category growth opportunities while suffering from industry-wide headwinds.

**Revenue Recovery Potential:** $468K-$735K through targeted initiatives in catering, to-go optimization, weeknight programming, service consistency, and signature item focus.

---

## Research Scope

This preliminary research encompasses four critical analyses:

1. **Brand Analysis** - Website audit and brand positioning assessment
2. **Competitor Analysis** - 19 local competitors analyzed via Google Places data
3. **Category Trends** - Casual dining Mexican restaurant industry dynamics

**Research Methodology:**
- Web scraping (El Patrón website)

## Category Trends Analysis: Industry Dynamics

### Trend 1: Dine-In Traffic Decline ❌
**Industry:** -8% to -12% full-service traffic (2023-2025)
**Casual Dining:** -10% to -15% (hit hardest)
**El Patrón:** -18.9% revenue (worse than industry average)

**Root Causes:**
- Post-pandemic behavioral shifts
- Economic pressure on middle-income families
- Competition from fast-casual (Chipotle, Moe's)
- Younger demographics prefer QSR or upscale

**Implication:** Cannot rely on dine-in recovery—must pivot to off-premise

---

### Trend 2: Bar/Alcohol Sales Collapse ❌
**Industry:** Margarita programs especially vulnerable
**El Patrón:** -$157K bar sales (-34% liquor, -25% beer)

**Contributing Factors:**
- DUI awareness + rideshare costs
- Health-conscious consumers (less alcohol)
- Work-from-home reducing happy hour traffic
- Competition from craft cocktail bars

**Implication:** Reinvent bar program—can't rely on traditional happy hour

---

### Trend 3: Menu Complexity Crisis ❌
**Industry:** Average menu 200-350 items, streamlining to 120-180
**El Patrón:** 330+ items (need to cut 150 items = 45% reduction)

**Why This Matters:**
- High complexity = slower ticket times
- More waste, training difficulty, inconsistency
- Margin improvement: 3-5% potential

**Implication:** Menu reduction is critical for operational excellence

---

### Trend 4: Off-Premise Surge ✅ (MAJOR OPPORTUNITY)
**Industry:** To-go/delivery now 30-40% of sales (up from 10-15% pre-2020)
**Catering:** Growing 8-12% annually for independents
**El Patrón:** To-go 25% (below average), Catering 4% (massive gap)

**Implication:** Off-premise is THE growth vector—must execute better

---

### Trend 5: Signature Item Economics ✅ (LEVERAGE STRENGTH)
**Industry:** Focus on menu stars, eliminate laggards
**El Patrón:** ACP (4.8%), Cheese Dip (3.0%), Margaritas (2.3%)

**Implication:** Build menu around proven winners, create variations
