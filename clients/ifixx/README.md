## Project Overview

Complete lead generation system for IFIXX Charlotte, a family-owned handyman and remodeling company serving two distinct customer segments:

| Segment | Target | Lead Magnet | Goal |
|---------|--------|-------------|------|
| **B2C** | Charlotte Homeowners | Pricing Guide PDF | Service calls |
| **B2B** | Property Managers | Make-Ready Checklist | Preferred Pricing Program enrollment |

---

## Core Positioning

**Rejected:** 24/7 emergency service positioning
**Chosen:** Same-day service + Owner-direct contact

### Brand Voice
> "The reliability of a professional, the familiarity of a neighbor."

### Key Differentiators
1. **Same-day service available** - Call in the morning, service that afternoon
2. **Talk to the owners** - Jaime or Hamed directly, not a call center
3. **Honest pricing** - Know the cost before work starts
4. **Transparent pricing** - Only Charlotte handyman publishing price ranges

## B2B Preferred Pricing Program

| Tier | Qualification | Benefits |
|------|---------------|----------|
| **Regular Client** | Any PM we work with | 10% preferred rate + priority scheduling |
| **Volume Client** | 5+ properties | 15% preferred rate + direct 24/7 line to owners |
| **Partner Client** | Ongoing relationship | Fixed rates by job type + monthly billing |

### Segmentation Tags
- `tier-regular` → 1-4 properties
- `tier-volume` → 5-9 properties
- `tier-partner` → 10+ properties
