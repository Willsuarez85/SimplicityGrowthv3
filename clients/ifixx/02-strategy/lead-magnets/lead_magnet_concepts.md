## Executive Summary

Two lead magnet concepts designed to capture leads from IFIXX's dual customer segments: B2C homeowners and B2B property managers. Each lead magnet addresses specific pain points and bridges naturally to service calls or preferred pricing program enrollment.

---

## SEGMENT A: HOMEOWNERS (B2C)

### Primary Lead Magnet: "Charlotte Home Repair Pricing Guide"

**The Concept:** A comprehensive PDF guide showing typical price ranges for the 25 most common home repairs in Charlotte, NC.

**Format:** PDF Guide (8-12 pages)

**The Hook:**
> "Stop Guessing What Repairs Cost. Get Charlotte's Only Transparent Home Repair Pricing Guide — Free."

**Why This Works:**
- Addresses #1 pain point: "I don't know what it'll cost"
- No other Charlotte handyman publishes pricing (competitive advantage)
- Creates trust through transparency before they even call
- Self-qualifies serious buyers (price shoppers don't convert anyway)

**Contents:**
1. Cover page with IFIXX branding
2. "Why We're Publishing Our Prices" (trust-building intro)
3. Everyday Repairs Price Ranges (15 items)
   - Toilet repair/replacement: $150-$400
   - Faucet installation: $125-$300
   - Drywall patch (small): $75-$150
   - TV mounting: $100-$200
   - Outlet/switch replacement: $100-$175
   - Door repair/adjustment: $100-$250
   - Garbage disposal: $200-$400
   - Water heater flush: $125-$200
   - Pressure washing (deck): $150-$300
   - And 6 more common repairs
4. "When DIY Makes Sense vs. Call a Pro" (builds authority)
5. Red Flags: "5 Signs Your Contractor Is Overcharging"
6. About Ifixx + Meet Jaime & Hamed (with photos)
7. Final CTA: "Ready for your exact quote? Call Jaime: [number]"

**The Bridge:**
- Guide ends with: "These are typical ranges. For YOUR exact quote with no surprises, call Ifixx. We'll match or beat these prices."
- Creates price anchor — when Ifixx quotes within range, trust is confirmed

**Implementation:**
- Difficulty: Low
- Resources: Canva or simple PDF design
- Time: 2-3 hours to create
- Distribution: Landing page, Facebook ads, Nextdoor

## SEGMENT B: PROPERTY MANAGERS (B2B)

### Primary Lead Magnet: "Complete Make-Ready Checklist"

**The Concept:** A comprehensive checklist + Excel template for tenant turnover, covering every inspection point and repair category.

**Format:** PDF Checklist + Excel Template

**The Hook:**
> "The 127-Point Make-Ready Checklist Property Managers Use to Never Miss Another Turnover Item"

**Why This Works:**
- Addresses pain point: "Every property is different" + "I miss things during make-readys"
- Immediately useful (they'll use it on next turnover)
- Positions Ifixx as the expert who understands PM operations
- Excel template creates ongoing utility (they keep coming back to it)

**Contents:**

**PDF Checklist (12 pages):**
1. Cover page + "How to Use This Checklist"
2. Pre-Turnover Walk-Through Section
   - Document existing damage
   - Photo documentation guide
   - Tenant damage vs. normal wear
3. Kitchen (28 items)
   - Appliances, cabinets, counters, plumbing, electrical
4. Bathrooms (24 items)
   - Fixtures, plumbing, ventilation, caulking, mirrors
5. Living Areas (22 items)
   - Walls, floors, windows, doors, lighting
6. Bedrooms (18 items)
   - Closets, windows, outlets, ceiling fans
7. Exterior/Entry (20 items)
   - Locks, weatherstripping, porch, landscaping
8. Systems Check (15 items)
   - HVAC, water heater, electrical panel, smoke detectors
9. Final Walk-Through (10 items)
   - Cleaning verification, photo documentation, key handoff
10. About Ifixx Preferred Pricing Program
11. "One Contact for All Your Properties" CTA

**Excel Template:**
- Property info header
- Checkbox columns for each item
- Notes column for specific issues
- Cost estimate column
- Vendor assignment column
- Completion date tracking
- Summary dashboard tab

**The Bridge:**
- Checklist ends with: "Need help completing these repairs? Ask about our Property Manager Preferred Pricing Program."
- Landing page asks: "How many properties do you manage?" (qualifies for tier)
- Follow-up email introduces preferred pricing tiers

**Implementation:**
- Difficulty: Medium
- Resources: PDF design + Excel template creation
- Time: 4-6 hours to create comprehensive version
- Distribution: LinkedIn, direct outreach, PM associations
