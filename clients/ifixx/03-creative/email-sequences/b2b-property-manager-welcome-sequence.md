**Preview:** 15 properties, one vendor, no more callbacks

---

Quick story:

Sarah manages 15 rental properties in South Charlotte. When we first talked, she had a problem:

**The situation:**
- 4 different vendors for different repairs
- Average turnover: 8-10 days
- 2-3 callbacks per turnover (missed items)
- No consistent pricing — every quote was different

**What changed:**

Sarah started using our checklist on every turnover. More importantly, she started using Ifixx as her single vendor.

**The results (6 months later):**
- Average turnover: 5-6 days
- Callbacks: nearly zero (the checklist catches everything)
- One invoice per turnover instead of four
- 15% preferred rate on all work
- Same-day response when tenants call with emergencies

Her exact words: *"I used to spend half my week coordinating vendors. Now I make one call and it's handled."*

**The math:**

- 3 fewer vacancy days × $50/day rent = $150 saved per turnover
- Zero callbacks × $200 average callback cost = $200+ saved per turnover
- 15% discount on all work = hundreds more per year

She didn't change properties. She didn't change her process. She just consolidated to one reliable vendor with preferred pricing.

Could this work for your portfolio?

If they reply to Email 1 with property count:

| Response | Tag | Follow-up Customization |
|----------|-----|------------------------|
| 1-4 properties | `tier-regular` | Emphasize quality + reliability |
| 5-9 properties | `tier-volume` | Emphasize 15% savings + priority |
| 10+ properties | `tier-partner` | Emphasize fixed rates + monthly billing |

### Hot Lead Indicators

Move to priority follow-up list if:
- They reply with property count (any number)
- They click CTA in Email 4 (schedule call)
- They reply asking questions about pricing

### Phone Follow-up

For subscribers who:
1. Indicated 5+ properties AND
2. Opened 3+ emails AND
3. Clicked any CTA

→ Jaime or Hamed calls directly within 48 hours

### Tone Notes

- Professional but personal (first name, direct language)
- Numbers-focused (PMs care about ROI)
- No hype, no pressure
- Emphasize: one contact, consistent pricing, documentation
- Always mention: "You talk to the owners, not a call center"
