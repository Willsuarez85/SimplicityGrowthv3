## Email 3: Value (DIY vs Call a Pro)

**Send:** Day 5
**Subject:** When to DIY vs when to call (honest answer)
**Preview:** Some repairs you CAN handle. Here's the line.

---

Here's something most contractors won't tell you:

**Some repairs you don't need us for.**

Seriously. Here's my honest take:

**DIY These:**
- Replacing a toilet flapper ($5 part, 10 minutes)
- Tightening a loose doorknob
- Unclogging a drain with a plunger
- Changing air filters
- Resetting a tripped breaker

**Call a Pro:**
- Anything with the main water line (flooding risk)
- Electrical panel work (safety risk)
- Gas line repairs (explosion risk, obviously)
- Structural issues (walls, foundation)
- Anything you've already tried twice and failed

**The Gray Zone (Could Go Either Way):**
- Toilet replacement — DIY if you're handy, call if not
- Faucet installation — Same deal
- Drywall patches — Easy if small, harder if large
- TV mounting — Depends on your wall type

The real question: **How much is your time worth?**

That "simple" faucet install? Might take you 4 hours including the hardware store trips. Or we can do it in 45 minutes and you get your Saturday back.

No judgment either way. Just giving you the honest breakdown.

Keep this email. You'll want it when something breaks.

— Jaime

P.S. Check the pricing guide for any of these gray zone items. You'll see if the cost makes sense vs your time.
