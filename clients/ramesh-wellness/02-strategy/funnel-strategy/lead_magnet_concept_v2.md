## The 12 Questions

### Section 1: Energy Signals (Questions 1-4)

**Q1. The 3pm Crash**
*How often do you experience a significant energy drop between 2-4pm?*

- Never (0 pts)
- Occasionally (1 pt)
- 2-3 times per week (2 pts)
- Almost daily (3 pts)
- Daily, severe enough to affect my work (4 pts)

**Q2. Morning Launch**
*How do you feel in the first hour after waking up?*

- Refreshed and ready to go (0 pts)
- Fine after coffee (1 pt)
- Groggy, takes 1-2 hours to feel normal (2 pts)
- Exhausted, feel like I didn't sleep (3 pts)
- Dread getting out of bed (4 pts)

**Q3. Energy Variability**
*How would you describe your energy pattern throughout the day?*

- Consistent and steady (0 pts)
- Predictable peaks and valleys (1 pt)
- Unpredictable—fine one hour, crashed the next (2 pts)
- Generally low with occasional bursts (3 pts)
- Running on fumes most of the time (4 pts)

**Q4. Recovery**
*After a demanding workday, how quickly do you recover?*

- Same day, I bounce back easily (0 pts)
- Next morning I'm fine (1 pt)
- Takes a full weekend to reset (2 pts)
- Never feel fully recovered anymore (3 pts)
- Each week I feel more depleted (4 pts)

---

### Section 2: Cognitive Signals (Questions 5-8)

**Q5. Brain Fog**
*How often do you experience "brain fog" (difficulty concentrating, thinking through a problem, or finding words)?*

- Rarely or never (0 pts)
- Occasionally, usually when tired (1 pt)
- Several times per week (2 pts)
- Daily, impacting my work (3 pts)
- Constantly, I don't trust my own thinking anymore (4 pts)

**Q6. Memory**
*In the past 6 months, how has your memory been?*

- Sharp as ever (0 pts)
- Slightly worse, but manageable (1 pt)
- Noticeably declining—forgetting meetings, names, details (2 pts)
- Significantly worse, I rely on notes for everything (3 pts)
- I've worried something is seriously wrong (4 pts)

**Q7. Decision Fatigue**
*How do you feel about making decisions at work?*

- Confident and clear (0 pts)
- Takes more effort than it used to (1 pt)
- I avoid decisions when I can (2 pts)
- Even small decisions feel overwhelming (3 pts)
- I second-guess everything now (4 pts)

**Q8. Mental Clarity Timing**
*When do you experience your best mental clarity?*

- Throughout the day (0 pts)
- Mornings only (1 pt)
- Unpredictable—random windows of clarity (2 pts)
- Late at night when I should be sleeping (3 pts)
- I can't remember the last time I felt mentally sharp (4 pts)

---

### Section 3: Sleep Signals (Questions 9-10)

**Q9. Sleep Pattern**
*Which best describes your typical night?*

- Fall asleep easily, sleep through the night (0 pts)
- Take a while to fall asleep, but sleep through (1 pt)
- Wake up 1-2 times but fall back asleep (2 pts)
- Wake up at 2-4am and can't fall back asleep (3 pts)
- Wired at midnight, exhausted by morning (4 pts)

**Q10. Sleep Quality**
*How often do you wake up feeling truly rested?*

- Most mornings (0 pts)
- A few times per week (1 pt)
- Once or twice per week (2 pts)
- Rarely (3 pts)
- Can't remember the last time (4 pts)

---

### Section 4: Medical Experience (Questions 11-12)

**Q11. Doctor Response**
*When you've mentioned these symptoms to your doctor, what was the response?*

- They took it seriously and we're working on it (0 pts)
- They ran tests, everything came back "normal" (2 pts)
- They suggested it's "just stress" or "just aging" (3 pts)
- They offered antidepressants or sleep medication (3 pts)
- I haven't mentioned it because I know they'll dismiss it (4 pts)

**Q12. Current Solutions**
*What have you tried to address these symptoms?*

- Nothing yet, just started noticing them (0 pts)
- Exercise, diet changes, supplements (1 pt)
- Multiple supplements, sleep apps, lifestyle changes (2 pts)
- HRT (with mixed or no results) (3 pts)
- Everything—and nothing has worked consistently (4 pts)

## Technical Requirements

### Platform Options

| Platform | Pros | Cons | Cost |
|----------|------|------|------|
| **Typeform** | Beautiful UX, logic branching | Limited reporting | $35-70/mo |
| **ScoreApp** | Built for quizzes, detailed analytics | Learning curve | $99/mo |
| **LeadQuizzes** | Good templates, built-in email | Less customizable | $49/mo |
| **Custom (Webflow + Airtable)** | Full control, on-brand | Dev time | $30+/mo |

**Recommendation:** ScoreApp for MVP (best balance of features and cost)
