## DESIGN DIRECTION

### Visual Style
- Clean, sophisticated, executive-appropriate
- No stock photos of women in suits looking stressed
- Consider: Abstract patterns, data visualizations, subtle medical imagery
- Color palette: Trust-building (blues, greens) with warmth accents

### Typography
- Headlines: Modern serif (authority)
- Body: Clean sans-serif (readability)
- Hierarchy clear for skimmers

### Imagery If Used
- Avoid: Clichéd "woman at desk with headache"
- Consider: Abstract representations of signals, patterns, rhythms
- Option: No imagery, let copy carry the weight
