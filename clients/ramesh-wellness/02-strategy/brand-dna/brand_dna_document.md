## Executive Summary

Ramesh Wellness Coaching is a personal brand coaching practice targeting professional women in corporate and finance sectors experiencing perimenopause and menopause. The brand combines functional medicine principles with an executive-friendly approach, positioning Ramesh as "the coach who speaks your language" for high-achieving women navigating hormonal transitions.

## 4. Brand Archetypes

### 4.1 Primary Archetype: The Sage

**Expression:** Ramesh is the trusted expert who cuts through confusion with clarity. She provides wisdom backed by evidence, making complex hormonal science accessible and actionable.

**In Practice:**
- Content educates before it sells
- Uses data and research citations
- Explains the "why" behind recommendations
- Positions as guide, not guru

### 4.2 Secondary Archetype: The Caregiver

**Expression:** Despite the data-driven approach, Ramesh genuinely cares about client wellbeing. The coaching relationship is supportive, not clinical.

**In Practice:**
- Warm, understanding communication
- Celebrates client wins
- Available and responsive
- Creates safe space for vulnerability

### 4.3 Archetype Balance

```
Sage (70%) ←————————→ Caregiver (30%)

"I have the expertise you need"  +  "And I genuinely care about your success"
```

## 9. Brand Guidelines Summary

### 9.1 Always Do

- Lead with education, not selling
- Use data and research to support claims
- Acknowledge the real challenges of menopause
- Respect the intelligence of the audience
- Offer actionable insights, not just information
- Speak to corporate/professional context
- Be warm but not overly casual

### 9.2 Never Do

- Make medical claims or diagnoses
- Promise specific outcomes or cures
- Use fear-based marketing
- Talk down to the audience
- Ignore the professional context
- Be overly clinical or cold
- Use "woo-woo" language without science backing
