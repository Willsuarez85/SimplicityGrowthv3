## 1. Visual Identity Overview

Ramesh Wellness Coaching's visual identity reflects the brand's position as a **sophisticated, science-backed wellness authority** for professional women. The aesthetic balances clinical credibility with warmth, appealing to executive women who value both evidence and approachability.

### Visual Equation

```
CLINICAL CREDIBILITY + WARM SOPHISTICATION + EXECUTIVE POLISH = RAMESH VISUAL IDENTITY

Clean, evidence-based    Approachable,        Premium, professional
aesthetic            +   not sterile      +   presentation
```

### 2.4 Color Psychology

**Deep Teal:** Combines the trustworthiness of blue with the healing energy of green. Signals expertise without being cold. Associated with wellness, balance, and wisdom.

**Warm Cream:** Creates a soft, welcoming foundation that avoids the sterility of pure white. Suggests approachability and comfort.

**Soft Sage:** Reinforces the wellness/health positioning while adding natural sophistication.

**Blush Rose:** Adds subtle femininity without being overly "girly"—appropriate for professional women.

### 2.5 Color Ratios

```
Primary (Deep Teal):      20%  - Key elements, CTAs
Neutral (Cream/White):    60%  - Backgrounds, breathing room
Secondary (Sage/Rose):    15%  - Accents, visual interest
Text (Charcoal):           5%  - Typography
```

## 3. Typography

### 3.1 Primary Typeface: Playfair Display

**Usage:** Headlines, titles, hero text
**Weight:** Bold (700) for headlines, Regular (400) for subheads
**Style:** Elegant serif that signals sophistication and authority

```css
font-family: 'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif;
```

**Example:**
# Performance Optimization Through Hormonal Health

### 3.2 Secondary Typeface: Inter

**Usage:** Body text, captions, UI elements
**Weight:** Regular (400), Medium (500), Semibold (600)
**Style:** Clean, highly readable sans-serif for professional accessibility

```css
font-family: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif;
```

**Example:**
Research shows that cortisol levels impact progesterone production—here's what that means for you.

### 3.3 Type Scale

| Element | Font | Size | Weight | Line Height |
|---------|------|------|--------|-------------|
| H1 - Hero | Playfair Display | 48-64px | Bold | 1.2 |
| H2 - Section | Playfair Display | 36-42px | Bold | 1.3 |
| H3 - Subsection | Inter | 24-28px | Semibold | 1.4 |
| Body | Inter | 16-18px | Regular | 1.6 |
| Caption | Inter | 14px | Regular | 1.5 |
| Button | Inter | 16px | Medium | 1.0 |

### 3.4 Typography Hierarchy

```
PLAYFAIR DISPLAY (Serif) - Authority, Expertise
├── H1: Hero statements, page titles
├── H2: Major section headers
└── Pull quotes, testimonials

INTER (Sans-Serif) - Clarity, Accessibility
├── H3-H6: Subsections, lists
├── Body text
├── Navigation, UI
└── CTAs, buttons
```

## 4. Visual Style Direction

### 4.1 Photography Style

**Subject Matter:**
- Professional women in executive settings (boardrooms, offices)
- Confident, accomplished women 45-65 years old
- Wellness moments that feel executive (yoga in upscale settings, healthy meals with wine glasses)
- Scientific/medical imagery that's approachable (labs with warm lighting)

**Aesthetic Qualities:**

| DO | DON'T |
|----|-------|
| Natural lighting, soft shadows | Harsh clinical lighting |
| Warm color grading | Cold, sterile tones |
| Real women (not overly styled) | Stock photo perfection |
| Professional settings | Gym/fitness clichés |
| Sophisticated wellness | "Woo-woo" imagery (crystals, etc.) |
| Diversity in age, ethnicity | Only young models |

**Example Descriptions:**

✅ **Good:** A woman in her 50s, silver-streaked hair, wearing a tailored blazer, reviewing documents in a sunlit office with a green smoothie nearby. Expression is focused, confident.

❌ **Bad:** A woman in workout clothes doing yoga on a beach at sunset with candles and crystals.

### 4.2 Imagery Keywords for AI Generation

**For fal.ai prompts, include:**

```

- editorial photography
- natural lighting
- warm color palette (teal, cream, sage accents)
- sophisticated, professional
- wellness without "woo-woo"
- executive setting
- confident mature woman

Technical keywords:
- soft shadows
- shallow depth of field
- clean composition
- minimal props
- high-end editorial quality
```

### 4.3 Illustration Style (If Used)

**Style:** Clean, minimalist line art or soft watercolor
**Colors:** Use brand palette (teal, sage, rose)
**Subjects:** Hormones/molecules (simplified), body systems, health concepts
**Avoid:** Overly cute, cartoony, or childish illustrations

## 5. Graphic Elements

### 5.1 Logo Usage

**Primary Logo:** "Ramesh Wellness" wordmark in Playfair Display
**Secondary:** "RW" monogram for social avatars

**Clear Space:** Minimum padding equal to the height of the "R"

**Minimum Size:**
- Digital: 120px width
- Print: 1 inch width

### 5.2 Icon Style

**Style:** Outlined, minimal stroke weight (1.5-2px)
**Corners:** Rounded (2px radius)
**Color:** Deep Teal primary, Charcoal secondary

**Recommended Icons:**
- Hormone molecule (simplified)
- Brain/cognition
- Calendar/clock (efficiency)
- Graph/data (evidence)
- Conversation bubble (coaching)
- Checkmark (results)

### 5.3 Patterns & Textures

**Primary Pattern:** Subtle organic wave (represents hormonal cycles)
- Use sparingly as background accent
- 10-15% opacity over Cream background

**Texture:** Paper grain texture
- Adds warmth to digital presentations
- Use at 5% opacity maximum

### 5.4 Data Visualization

**Style:** Clean, minimal charts and graphs
**Colors:** Deep Teal for primary data, Sage for secondary, Rose for highlights
**Typography:** Inter Medium for labels

## 6. Layout Principles

### 6.1 Grid System

**Web/Digital:** 12-column grid
**Margins:** 5-10% on each side
**Gutter:** 24-32px

**Mobile:** Single column, generous padding

### 6.2 White Space

**Philosophy:** Generous white space signals premium quality and sophistication. Executive audience appreciates visual breathing room.

**Minimum Margins:**
- Sections: 80-120px vertical spacing
- Paragraphs: 24px spacing
- Elements: 16px minimum

### 6.3 Visual Hierarchy

```
1. Hero statement (what you notice first)
2. Primary CTA (what you do next)
3. Supporting content (why you should)
4. Secondary CTAs (additional options)
5. Footer/navigation
```

## 7. Platform-Specific Guidelines

### 7.1 YouTube

**Thumbnails:**
- Clean composition, 2-3 elements maximum
- Face visible (if featuring Ramesh)
- Text: 3-5 words, Playfair Display Bold
- Colors: High contrast (Deep Teal text on Cream, or reverse)
- Emotion: Confident, approachable expression

**Video Aesthetic:**
- Clean, uncluttered background
- Warm, natural lighting
- Teal accent in setting (plant, cushion, mug)
- Professional but not sterile

### 7.2 LinkedIn

**Post Images:**
- Professional, executive aesthetic
- Data/graphs styled in brand colors
- Quotes in Playfair Display on Cream backgrounds
- Headshot with Teal overlay for profile

**Cover Image:**
- Clean, editorial photography
- Tagline in Playfair Display
- Subtle brand pattern overlay

### 7.3 Instagram

**Feed Aesthetic:**
- Cohesive grid pattern
- Alternating Cream and white backgrounds
- Consistent filter (warm, slight desaturation)
- Mix: 60% educational graphics, 30% photos, 10% testimonials

**Stories:**
- Brand colors only
- Inter for text (readable on mobile)
- Simple animations (subtle)

### 7.4 Google Ads / Landing Pages

**Visual Priority:**
1. Clear, compelling headline
2. Professional photography or illustration
3. Trust signals (credentials, testimonials)
4. Strong, teal CTA button

**Style:**
- Maximum clarity
- Minimal visual noise
- White/Cream backgrounds
- Single, focused action
