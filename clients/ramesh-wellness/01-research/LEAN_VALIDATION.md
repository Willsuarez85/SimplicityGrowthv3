### Customer Acquisition Economics

**Conversion Funnel (industry standard):**
- 100 website visitors
- → 10 lead magnet downloads (10% opt-in)
- → 5 discovery calls booked (50% of leads)
- → 1 paying client (20% close rate)
- **Overall conversion:** 1%

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):**
- **Organic/SEO:** $0-50 per client (time investment, no ad spend)
- **Paid Ads (Google):** CPC $4.70 avg × 100 clicks = $470 per client
- **Blended CAC (60% organic, 40% paid):** $200-300

**LTV:CAC Ratio:**
- **Best Case (organic-heavy):** $900 / $200 = **4.5:1** ✅
- **Base Case (blended):** $900 / $300 = **3.0:1** ✅
- **Worst Case (paid-heavy):** $900 / $470 = **1.9:1** ⚠️

**Industry Benchmark:** >3:1 is healthy, >2:1 is acceptable.
