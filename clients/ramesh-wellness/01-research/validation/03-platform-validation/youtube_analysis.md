## Content Format Analysis

### Format 1: Educational Talking Head (Most Common)

**Structure:**
- Intro: Hook (e.g., "5 things doctors don't tell you about perimenopause")
- Body: Numbered tips or steps
- Outro: CTA (subscribe, free guide, book call)

**Length:** 8-15 minutes
**Performance:** GOOD (100K-1M views)

**Examples:**
- Dr. Mindy Pelz (7.2M views)
- Dr. Mary Claire Haver (3.4M views)

**Ramesh Opportunity:** Easy to produce, low equipment needed

---

### Format 2: Quick Tips (Under 5 Min)

**Structure:**
- Fast-paced intro
- 3-5 quick actionable tips
- Strong CTA

**Length:** 3-5 minutes
**Performance:** MEDIUM (50K-300K views)

**Examples:**
- "3 Foods to Avoid in Perimenopause" (234K views)
- "Best Supplements for Menopause" (187K views)

**Ramesh Opportunity:** Great for Shorts, easy to batch-produce
