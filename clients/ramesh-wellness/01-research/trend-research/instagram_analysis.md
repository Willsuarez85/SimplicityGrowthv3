## Instagram vs. YouTube Comparison

| Metric | Instagram | YouTube |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| **Discovery Potential** | Medium (hashtags, explore) | High (search, suggested) |
| **Content Depth** | Low (15-60 sec, carousels) | High (8-15 min videos) |
| **Engagement Style** | Quick saves, likes | Longer watch time, comments |
| **Lead Quality** | Medium (broader audience) | High (active problem-solvers) |
| **Production Effort** | Low (phone, quick edits) | Medium (setup, longer edits) |
| **Longevity** | Low (24-48 hour lifespan) | High (evergreen search traffic) |

**Strategy Integration:**
- **Instagram:** Top-of-funnel awareness, quick tips, community building
- **YouTube:** Middle-of-funnel education, authority building, SEO
- **LinkedIn:** Bottom-of-funnel credibility, corporate positioning
- **Website:** Conversion (discovery calls, email capture)
