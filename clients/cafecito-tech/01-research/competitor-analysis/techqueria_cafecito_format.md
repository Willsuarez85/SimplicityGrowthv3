**Research Date:** 2026-01-10

## Executive Summary

Techqueria operates the largest Latinx tech community in the U.S. with 16,000+ members. Their "Cafecito" format represents a low-commitment, high-frequency model for community engagement. The weekly 1-hour virtual cafecitos, combined with specialized tracks (UX Cafecito, Product Cafecito, Cyber Cafecito), provide a scalable community structure that maintains engagement without resource-intensive event production.

## Cafecito Event Format

### Core Concept

"Cafecito" = casual coffee conversation format
- Low-pressure networking
- Topic-focused discussion
- Community-building through regularity

### Virtual Cafecito Structure

**Schedule:**
- Frequency: Weekly (Fridays)
- Time: 10:00 AM - 11:00 AM PST
- Duration: 1 hour
- Format: Virtual (Zoom/similar)

**Session Flow (Typical):**
1. Welcome and introductions (10 min)
2. Topic discussion or speaker (30 min)
3. Open networking/Q&A (20 min)

### Specialized Cafecito Tracks

Techqueria has developed role-specific cafecitos:

| Track | Focus | Audience |
|-------|-------|----------|
| UX Cafecito | UX design, research, tools | UXers, researchers, designers |
| Product Cafecito | Product management, strategy | Product managers |
| Cyber Cafecito | Cybersecurity, InfoSec | Security professionals |
| Data Cafecito | Data science, analytics | Data professionals |
| Engineering Cafecito | Software development | Engineers |

**UX Cafecito Description (Direct Quote):**
"A time for UXers, researchers, and anyone interested in UX to get together to chat, meet other Latinx UXers, and help each other with questions/advice."

### In-Person Cafecitos

Beyond virtual programming:
- City-based meetups (Austin, Miami, San Diego, SF, LA, Seattle, NYC)
- Local chapter-organized events
- Hybrid format options

## Operational Model

### Low-Resource Requirements

The cafecito model succeeds with minimal resources:

| Resource | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Venue (virtual) | Zoom/video platform |
| Host | 1 volunteer facilitator |
| Planning | 1-2 hours per event |
| Promotion | Slack + LinkedIn posts |
| Cost | Near-zero (virtual) |

### Scalability Factors

What makes this model scale:
1. **Low barrier to host:** Any community member can facilitate
2. **Repeatable format:** Same structure week after week
3. **Virtual-first:** No venue logistics
4. **Role-based tracks:** Distributed leadership
5. **Community-driven content:** Topics emerge from members

## Key Differentiators

### Techqueria vs. Cafecito Tech

| Dimension | Techqueria | Cafecito Tech |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Scale | National (16K+ members) | Local (Charlotte) |
| Format | Virtual-first | In-person first |
| Frequency | Weekly | Monthly |
| Scope | All Latinx in tech | Latino professionals + tech allies |
| Chapter Model | City chapters | Single location (initially) |

### What Cafecito Tech Can Learn

1. **Consistency matters:** Weekly/monthly regularity builds habit
2. **Specialized tracks:** Role-based programming increases relevance
3. **Low-commitment entry:** 1-hour cafecitos reduce barrier
4. **ERG partnerships:** Corporate Latino ERGs are natural allies
5. **Slack community:** Between-event engagement maintains connection

## Strategic Observations

### Techqueria Success Factors

1. **Frequency:** Weekly touchpoints maintain engagement
2. **Specialization:** Role-based tracks serve specific needs
3. **Virtual scale:** Geography doesn't limit growth
4. **ERG leverage:** Corporate partners provide resources
5. **Community ownership:** Members drive content and chapters

### Potential Challenges for Cafecito Tech

1. **In-person only:** Higher logistics burden
2. **Monthly cadence:** Less frequent touchpoints
3. **Local focus:** Smaller potential audience
4. **Resource needs:** Requires sponsor funding for venue/food

### Opportunity

Cafecito Tech's in-person focus is a **strength** in Charlotte's context:
- Virtual fatigue persists post-COVID
- No local in-person Latino tech events exist
- Face-to-face networking creates stronger bonds
- Sponsors prefer visibility at physical events
