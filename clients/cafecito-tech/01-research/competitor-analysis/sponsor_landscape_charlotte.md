# Sponsor Landscape Charlotte: Corporate Partnership Opportunities

**Research Date:** 2026-01-10
**Analyst:** Market & Competitor Analyst
**Client:** Cafecito Tech (Link Foundation)
**Scope:** Potential corporate sponsors, Latino ERGs, and partnership opportunities in Charlotte market

---

## Executive Summary

Charlotte's corporate landscape offers significant sponsorship potential for Cafecito Tech. The city's status as a major banking center means large employers with established Latino ERGs, dedicated DEI budgets, and recruiting needs for diverse talent. The absence of competing Latino tech events means sponsors have no alternative outlet for community engagement in this demographic.

## Sponsorship Package Framework

### Recommended Tier Structure

Based on Tech in Motion model adapted for Charlotte market:

#### Presenting Partner ($5,000/year)

**Investment:** $5,000 annual commitment

**Benefits:**
- Title sponsorship of 6 events ("Cafecito Tech presented by [Company]")
- Logo prominence on all materials
- Speaking slot at 2 events
- Premium positioning in all communications
- First access to attendee database (opt-in)
- Dedicated social media recognition
- Booth/table at all events
- 10 reserved seats per event

**Best For:** Anchor sponsors seeking year-round visibility (Bank of America, Wells Fargo)

---

#### Event Sponsor ($1,000/event)

**Investment:** $1,000 per event

**Benefits:**
- Event co-branding
- Speaking introduction (1-2 minutes)
- Logo on event materials
- Booth/table at event
- 4 reserved seats
- Post-event attendee list access (opt-in)
- Social media recognition

**Best For:** Companies testing partnership before annual commitment

---

#### Community Partner ($500/event)

**Investment:** $500 per event

**Benefits:**
- Logo placement on materials
- 2 reserved seats
- Recognition in opening remarks
- Social media mention
- Networking access

**Best For:** Smaller companies, startups, or ERG-only sponsorships

## Sponsorship Value Proposition

### For Corporate Sponsors

**ROI Arguments:**

1. **Talent Pipeline**
   - Average tech hire cost: $15,000-25,000
   - If sponsorship leads to 1 quality hire, ROI positive
   - Direct access to diverse candidates

2. **DEI Metrics**
   - Demonstrable community investment
   - ERG engagement activity
   - Board-reportable partnership

3. **Employee Engagement**
   - Support internal Latino employees
   - Professional development opportunity
   - Retention benefit

4. **Brand Visibility**
   - 150-300 professionals per event
   - LinkedIn amplification
   - Community goodwill

### Testimonial Framework (Future Use)

*"Sponsoring Cafecito Tech has been pivotal to [Company]'s success in building relationships with Charlotte's Latino tech community. The quality of events is excellent and the team is easy to work with. We truly value the partnership."*

## Risk Factors and Mitigation

### Sponsorship Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Economic downturn cuts DEI budgets | Medium | Start with ERG relationships (lower budget impact) |
| Key contact leaves company | Medium | Build multiple relationships at each company |
| Sponsor expects unrealistic ROI | Medium | Set clear expectations, document outcomes |
| Competition emerges | Low | Lock in long-term partnerships early |

### Relationship Management

**Best Practices:**
1. Regular communication (monthly updates)
2. Post-event reporting with metrics
3. Recognition at events
4. Involvement in programming decisions
5. Renewal conversations 3 months before expiration

Charlotte's sponsor landscape is highly favorable for Cafecito Tech:

2. **No competing events** for Latino tech demographic
3. **Strong DEI budgets** at financial services companies
4. **Clear value proposition** for talent and engagement

**Primary Recommendation:** Pursue Bank of America HOLA ERG as anchor sponsor through Link Foundation network connections.
