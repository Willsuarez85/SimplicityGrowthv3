# Local Event Gap Analysis: Charlotte Latino Tech Market

**Research Date:** 2026-01-10
**Analyst:** Market & Competitor Analyst
**Client:** Cafecito Tech (Link Foundation)
**Scope:** Market gap analysis for Latino tech community events in Charlotte

---

## Executive Summary

Charlotte's tech ecosystem demonstrates robust activity across general networking, startup programming, and specialized user groups. However, a critical gap exists: **no Latino-focused tech networking events operate in the Charlotte market**. This represents a clear, uncontested opportunity for Cafecito Tech to establish the first and only Latino tech professional community in the region.

## Latino Representation Gap Analysis

### Current Latino Participation in Charlotte Tech Events

**Observed:** Latino attendance at general Charlotte tech events appears minimal based on:
- Event imagery and promotional materials
- Organizer/speaker demographics
- No Spanish-language content or bilingual programming
- No cultural identity components

**Structural Barriers:**
1. Events designed for majority culture preferences
2. No representation in leadership or speakers
3. Timing conflicts with cultural/family obligations
4. No visible pathway for Latino community involvement

4. **Corporate Blind Spot:** Charlotte corporations have ERGs but no external partner

The gap analysis confirms a clear, uncontested market opportunity for Cafecito Tech.
